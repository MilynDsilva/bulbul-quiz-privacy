# BulBul Quiz — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

BulBul Quiz ("the app") is a trivia game. This policy explains what happens to your information.

## Information we collect
**None.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or share any personal information.

## Information stored on your device
Your quiz answers, scores and coins are saved **only on your own device** so you can continue where you left off. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are deleted if you uninstall the app or tap *Reset all progress*.

## Sharing
If you tap *Share* on a result, your phone's own share menu opens with a short message about your score. You choose whether and where to send it; the app itself sends nothing.

## Permissions
The app needs no special permissions.

## Children
The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this address with a new date.
