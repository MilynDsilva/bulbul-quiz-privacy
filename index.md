# BulBul Quiz — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 October 2026_

BulBul Quiz ("the app") is a trivia game. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

## No accounts, no ads
There is no sign-up or sign-in and no advertising. We never ask for your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

## Information stored on your device
Your quiz answers, scores and coins are saved **only on your device** so you can continue where you left off. They are deleted if you uninstall the app or tap *Reset all progress*.

## Anonymous usage analytics
To understand which quizzes people enjoy and to improve the app, BulBul Quiz uses **Google Analytics for Firebase**. It collects:
- **App activity** — screens viewed, quizzes started, finished or left, answers marked right or wrong, quizzes unlocked, results shared, and scores.
- **Device and app information** — app version, device model, operating system version, language and approximate (country/city-level) location derived from your network.
- **An app-instance identifier** — a random ID generated by Firebase for this installation (not linked to your name or account).

This data is used only for analytics, is not used for advertising, and is not sold. It is processed by Google as our service provider under the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and is retained in Google Analytics for up to 14 months.

**You can turn this off at any time:** open the app → **Me** → switch off **Share anonymous usage data**.

## Sharing
If you tap *Share* on a result, your phone's share menu opens with a short message about your score. You choose whether and where to send it.

## Children
The app is suitable for everyone, but it is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices and requests
You can switch off analytics in the app (Me tab), reset local progress, or uninstall the app. To ask about or request deletion of analytics data linked to your installation, email us.
